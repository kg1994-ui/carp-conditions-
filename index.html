How the score works

Carpcast scores each day from 0 to 100 using real carp catches logged by Dutch anglers from 2009 to 2024, matched to the weather on the day.

Weather 60% · temperature, wind and pressure across your local area, for the time of year
Falling pressure 15% · a drop since the day before
Moon 25% · best around new and full moon

Slow 0–24 · Fair 25–44 · Good 45–64 · Very good 65–79 · Big carp 80+

Weather comes from Open-Meteo, a free service that brings together forecasts from the UK Met Office and other national weather agencies. Beyond the 16-day forecast, Carpcast shows what's typical from 20 years of past weather.
